1 The papers at a glance

You sit two papers in the same tier. Both papers cover the whole specification, and you may use a calculator in both.

Foundation tier

  • Papers 1F and 2F
  • Time 2 hours each
  • Marks 100 each (50% of the grade each)
  • Grades 5 to 1
  • Calculator allowed in both

Higher tier

  • Papers 1H and 2H
  • Time 2 hours each
  • Marks 100 each (50% of the grade each)
  • Grades 9 to 4 (a grade 3 is allowed if you only just miss a 4)
  • Calculator allowed in both

Roughly 1 mark per minute: a 4-mark question deserves about 4 minutes. A formulae sheet is printed inside every paper (see section 2).

2 The formulae sheet

These formulae are given to you on the inside cover of the paper. You do not need to memorise them, but you must know when to use each one.

Area of a trapezium

\[A = \tfrac12 (a + b)h\]

Volume of a prism

\[V = A \times l\]\(A\) = area of the cross section, \(l\) = length

Cylinder

\[V = \pi r^2 h\]\[\text{Curved surface area} = 2\pi r h\]

Arithmetic series H

\[S_n = \tfrac{n}{2}\,[\,2a + (n - 1)d\,]\]

Quadratic formula H

The solutions of \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\), where \(a \ne 0\):\[x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\]

Trigonometry in any triangle H

\[\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}\]\[a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A\]\[\text{Area} = \tfrac12 ab\sin C\]

Cone H

\[V = \tfrac13 \pi r^2 h\]\[\text{Curved surface area} = \pi r l\]

Sphere H

\[V = \tfrac43 \pi r^3\]\[\text{Surface area} = 4\pi r^2\]

3 Formulae you must learn by heart

These are not on the formulae sheet, but they come up in almost every paper.

Circles

\[C = \pi d = 2\pi r\]\[A = \pi r^2\]

Pythagoras' theorem

\[a^2 + b^2 = c^2\]c is the hypotenuse, opposite the right angle.

Right-angled trigonometry

\[\sin\theta = \frac{O}{H}\qquad \cos\theta = \frac{A}{H}\]\[\tan\theta = \frac{O}{A}\]

Compound measures

\[\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}\]\[\text{density} = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}\]\[\text{pressure} = \frac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}\]

Area of shapes

\[\text{rectangle} = lw\]\[\text{triangle} = \tfrac12 bh\]\[\text{parallelogram} = bh\]

Angles in polygons

Sum of the interior angles:\[(n - 2) \times 180^\circ\]Each exterior angle of a regular polygon:\[\frac{360^\circ}{n}\]

Straight lines

\[y = mx + c\]\[m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\]

Percentages

Percentage change:\[\frac{\text{change}}{\text{original}} \times 100\]Compound growth after n years:\[P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n\]

Probability

\[P(\text{not } A) = 1 - P(A)\]Expected frequency:\[P(A) \times \text{trials}\]

Arcs and sectors H

\[\text{arc} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r\]\[\text{sector} = \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2\]

Differentiation H

\[y = ax^n \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{dy}{dx} = nax^{n-1}\]Stationary points where \(\frac{dy}{dx} = 0\).

Similar shapes H

Scale factor k:\[\text{lengths} \times k\]\[\text{areas} \times k^2\]\[\text{volumes} \times k^3\]

6 Exam technique

✍️ Show your working

Most marks are for the method. A correct method with a slip at the end still earns most of the marks. A wrong answer with no working earns nothing.

🎯 Read the question twice

Underline what it asks for: the units, “give your answer to 3 significant figures”, “in its simplest form”, “show that”.

🧮 Use your calculator well

Write the full calculator value first, then round. Keep exact values (fractions, \(\pi\), surds) until the last step.

⏱️ About 1 mark a minute

If you are stuck, move on and come back later. Leave time at the end to check your answers.

📏 Bring the right kit

Black pen, pencil, ruler, protractor, compasses, eraser and a scientific calculator.

🔍 Check it makes sense

Estimate first. Is the answer a sensible size? A probability must be between 0 and 1, and a length cannot be negative.
